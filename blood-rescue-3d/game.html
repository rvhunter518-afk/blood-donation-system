How to Play

  • W A S D — Move around the hospital
  • Mouse — Look around (click screen to lock camera)
  • E — Interact with patients, donors, and rooms
  • Read the patient's blood requirement at mission start
  • Find a donor whose blood group matches the requirement
  • Escort the suitable donor to the Blood Collection Room and collect blood
  • Deliver the collected blood to the Emergency Room before time runs out

About

Blood Rescue 3D is an educational mini-game built as an extra feature for the Blood Connect blood-donation website. It simulates a fictional emergency blood-rescue scenario to teach the basics of blood group compatibility in a fun, gamified way.

⚠ This game is an educational simulation using fictional characters and scenarios. It is not a medical decision-making tool.
